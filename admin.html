Report types

Base
Required. Daily account/campaign/ad data. All totals, WoW, MoM, SKU, campaign and creative economics use only Base rows.
Demographic
Age and Gender breakdowns. This powers age, gender and age × gender views.
Geo
Country, Region, City and DMA. Keep one geography basis consistent per export.
Delivery
Platform, Network, Placement and Device. Meta and Google fields can coexist.
Google_Search
Keyword, Search Term, Match Type and search impression-share diagnostics.
Creative_Extra
Optional creative format, objective, video 3-second views and ThruPlays.

What to pull

Meta
Base daily export plus separate breakdown exports for Age + Gender, Country/Region/DMA, Publisher Platform/Placement/Device. Keep purchase, spend, impressions, link clicks, LPV, ATC, checkout and purchase value.
Google
Base daily campaign/ad-group export, demographics, locations, devices/networks, plus Search Terms/Keywords with match type, cost, clicks, purchases, conversion value and impression-share metrics.
Do not combine grains
A demographic row is not a Base row. A device row is not a Base row. Report_Type is what prevents duplicate spend and purchases in the executive totals.

Required core fields

SKU is strongly recommended. Optional metrics can remain blank or 0. Google LPV, ATC and Checkout can remain 0 until those events are imported into Google Ads.
