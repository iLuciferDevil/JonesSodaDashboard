Performance trajectory

The account had a strong acquisition baseline through August. September changed the economics: CPA moved above the $15 guardrail, ROAS compressed, and the latest complete week reached $27.82 CPA.

Operating interpretation

The deterioration has multiple drivers. Rocket shows audience and creative pressure alongside post-click weakness. Orange 4-Pack retains click interest, with a larger share of the loss occurring after the landing page.

What worked and what did not

Evidence-based read from the latest complete week plus the month-over-month pattern.

What worked

Google is currently inside the guardrail
Current Google blended CPA is $8.12 and Fallout campaigns are at $9.78, so the issue is concentrated in Meta recovery rather than a universal demand failure.
August proved the ceiling
Account CPA was $5.16 in August with 8,777 purchases and 14.25x ROAS. The platform can acquire efficiently when audience and SKU momentum line up.
4-Pack still has workable creative pockets
In the latest week, Dog Tag Ad 4 V2 delivered $12.53 CPA and Dog Tag Ad 3 V2 delivered $12.76 CPA at smaller spend levels, worth controlled continuation.
Soda audience is the cleaner Rocket pocket
Soda was still the least-bad Rocket audience in the latest complete week at $25.22 CPA versus $31.19 for Fallout Fans and $34.61 for the 1% Lookalike.

What did not work

Rocket CPA moved well above the $15 guardrail
Orange Rocket Bottle moved from $5.84 CPA in August to $16.94 in September and $31.17 in Oct MTD. Latest full week was $28.50.
4-Pack quality collapsed after the click
Orange 4-Pack was $6.04 CPA in August, then $25.04 in September. Purchase rate fell from 12.8% to 4.6%, while click interest did not fully disappear.
Weak audiences pulled blended CPA up
Latest week Rocket 1% Lookalike sat at $34.61 CPA and Fallout Fans at $31.19. These should be constrained until quality recovers.
Phase 2 Rocket creative produced attention without buyer quality
Phase 2 Ad 1 ran above $32 CPA across Rocket ad sets in the latest week. It should be replaced with approved photography-led work.

Age cut

45-64 is worth testing

45-54 CPA moved from $9.26 to $38.27 in the break window. 55-64 moved from $20.78 to $43.13. Test 18-44 versus all eligible ages after the first audience winner emerges.

Gender cut

No useful recovery split

Both male and female cohorts deteriorated materially. Gender does not provide a clean enough hypothesis to justify fragmenting the restart.

Warm traffic

Remarketing needs to re-earn spend

4-Pack remarketing entered the weak period at $25.16 CPA, then ran at $28.06. Warm traffic should be measured against cold, rather than assumed to be the efficient answer.

Rocket learning gate

CPA ≤ $18

LPV → Purchase should recover to at least 2.7% during learning. Scale only after 10+ purchases and a 7-day CPA at or below $15.

4-Pack learning gate

LPV → ATC ≥ 18%

CPA should stay at or below $18 during learning. Scale only when 7-day CPA reaches $15 and LPV → Purchase reaches at least 6%.

Pause review

$30 / $45 spend gates

Review at roughly 2x target CPA spend with weak downstream signal. Pause review at roughly 3x target CPA with no purchase or persistent funnel collapse.

Recovery plan for this week and the next

The restart is structured to produce answers quickly while protecting signal.

Day 0
Launch
Upload human photography, QA UTMs and purchase event, then launch ABO audience cells with identical creative.
Day 1-2
Protect signal
Watch delivery, LPV rate, ATC, checkout and purchase signal. Avoid premature winners.
Day 3-4
First cuts
Pause cells that breach spend guardrails with weak downstream behavior. Keep viable challengers funded.
Day 5-7
First reallocation
Shift incremental budget toward cells with improving CPA and post-click quality.
Week 2
Validate and expand
Run the age overlay on the winner, start hook tests, and re-test warm traffic only if the pool is large enough.
